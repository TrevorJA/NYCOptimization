# Epsilon Calibration

> Outputs cited under `outputs/supplemental/epsilon_calibration/` were archived on 2026-11-04 (`archive/2026-11-04_pre_demand_update/`, pre-demand-update results); they regenerate under the current campaign.

Sets the annual-unit search epsilons, the values Borg's ε-dominance archive uses. `config.get_epsilons()` reads `src/objectives_ensemble._ANNUAL_REGISTRY_SPEC` at runtime and the MOEAFramework CLIs take `--epsilon` strings, so an epsilon change needs no JAR rebuild (the problem JARs carry only the DV and objective counts). An epsilon must resolve practically significant policy differences in native units, must sit at or above the measured floors of its objective (signal scale, estimator noise, granularity), and leaves the cardinality of the reported Pareto-approximate set to the post-search re-filter. The signal-scale rule follows Reed et al. (2013) and the noise-exclusion requirement Kasprzyk et al. (2013). Manuscript statement in Section 3.2.2 and SI Text S5.

## Adopted vector

`[0.025, 5.0, 0.025, 5.0, 5.0, 0.3, 5.0, 0.025]` in registry order (decision 2026-11-09), one shared precision per objective family: reliabilities 0.025 (2.5 percent of annual units, about two years of the 77-year historic record), deficit-P99s 5.0 % of target, flood exceedance 0.3 ft·d/yr, storage P01 5.0 % of capacity.

Each entry is the smallest difference judged practically significant for basin operations; finer values were rejected on that ground (a 1 % Trenton deficit distinction, for example, carries no operational meaning). The vector is deliberately finer than an archive-cardinality target alone would set: the search errs toward a large archive and the reported cardinality is fixed after the search by the Stage 2 re-filter, so an under-resolved front cannot be recovered without a new search, whereas an over-resolved one can be coarsened at no simulation cost.

Cardinality at this vector was estimated before adoption by re-filtering the union of every island-archive snapshot of the first HIST production seed (7,049 distinct solutions; `scripts/supplemental/epsilon_union_refilter.py`, launcher `workflow/supplemental/results_diagnostics/epsilon_union_refilter.sh`, outputs `outputs/supplemental/epsilon_union_refilter/`). The estimate is about 106 members on the historic trace, a lower bound because a search under a finer vector retains and explores more than the archive it is estimated from. Ensemble-design fronts are larger: their objectives are smoother and their reliabilities are not lattice-valued. On the historic trace the reliabilities lie on a 1/77 lattice, so 0.025 spans about two lattice steps; the Trenton deficit and flood exceedance axes span at most three boxes across the range the HIST search explored.

### Stage 1, per-objective floors

Scripts `scripts/supplemental/epsilon_calibration_{run,figures}.py`, launcher `workflow/supplemental/results_diagnostics/epsilon_calibration.sh` (one job per design, selected by `NYCOPT_ENV_FILE=workflow/envs/eps_calib_*.env`), settings `EPS_*` in `supplemental_config.py`, outputs `outputs/supplemental/epsilon_calibration/`.

- **Policies.** `EPS_N_POLICIES` = 512 random decision vectors drawn uniform on the constraint-feasible region (rejection against the two DV-space Borg constraints, acceptance rate persisted as QC) plus the FFMP baseline (id −1). Feasible-only sampling matches the archive's own population.
- **Evaluation.** Each policy runs once per design through the batched worker path (`run_simulation_ensemble_batched`, `EPS_REALIZATION_BATCH` = 150) on that design's own search ensemble, storing the stage-(i) cube `(n_dv × n_real × n_obj × n_units)` for the whole annual-unit registry (`cube/unit_cube_{formulation}_{design}_seed{S}_n{N}.h5`). Pooling the cube through the unit operator reproduces the search scalar exactly, and the framing-convention screens read the same cubes.
- **Floor.** Per objective, the clean-rounded maximum of (a) the signal scale, IQR of the search scalar across feasible policies divided by 10, (b) the noise floor, bootstrap SD of the unit-operator estimator under resampling of the realization axis (`EPS_BOOTSTRAP_B` = 1000, median across policies; the historic design resamples unit-years, an i.i.d. approximation that understates noise under serial dependence, disclosed), and (c) the granularity, the 1/(N·units) lattice of the frequency objectives. The floor is taken over `EPS_CAMPAIGN_DESIGNS` (monte_carlo, hazard_filling_stationary) with the binding design recorded; the raw requirement differs by at most 2.5× between them. The historic reference is measured and reported but excluded from the maximum, because its 77-unit estimator would coarsen the reliability precisions well beyond what the ensemble designs need. It searches at the shared vector, and its archive resolves below its own noise on the reliability axes (disclosed).
- **Campaign ensemble size.** Floors were measured on the N = 100 draw-0 ensembles and are not re-measured on the N = 300 campaign ensembles: the binding floors are noise floors, the bootstrap SD of the unit-operator estimator falls with the realization count, and every adopted entry sits at or above its N = 100 floor, so the N = 100 floors bound the N = 300 floors from above.
- **Demand at calibration (disclosure).** The stage-1 cubes were simulated with NYC demand held constant at its 800 MGD allocation, before the time-dynamic extrapolated series (`config.nyc_demand_source`) was adopted on 2026-11-03. The floors are not re-measured under the current series: the NYC delivery floors are noise and granularity floors, and the cubes are regenerated only if the framing-convention screens are re-run (`TODO.md` §0).

### Stage 2, post-search cardinality re-filter

The reported Pareto-approximate set of each design is the search archive re-filtered under a grouped vector, one ε per family. `scripts/supplemental/epsilon_ensemble_refilter.py` (launcher `workflow/supplemental/results_diagnostics/epsilon_ensemble_refilter.sh`, outputs `outputs/supplemental/epsilon_refilter/`) re-filters converged search archives (each seed's `.set` and the step-09 plain union) under grouped candidate vectors, with an exact ε-box filter cross-checked against `sensitivity_common.epsilon_nondominated` and Borg's own archive membership. The selection rule is a cap: the finest candidate whose largest-design retained union, inflated by the conservative 1.35 factor, is at or below `MAX_FRONT_SIZE` = 1,000 (`--max-size` overrides it), applied identically to every design and checked for axis coverage (`grouped_axis_coverage` tables and two-panel parallel-axes figures). The reliability family is the dominant cardinality lever and the deficit family the secondary one. When the search archives already sit under the cap, the search vector is the reported vector.

**Disclosures.** Static re-filter counts are lower bounds on live archives, because the ε-archive steers selection and restarts during a run; the measured live inflation is 10 to 35 %. `scripts/supplemental/epsilon_refilter_sweep.py` (launcher `epsilon_refilter_sweep.sh`) prices cardinality under scaled variants of one vector, and `scripts/supplemental/write_refiltered_sets.py` writes archives re-filtered under a new vector to new filenames, never over production artifacts.

## Merger behaviour

MOEAFramework v5 `ResultFileMerger` merges by plain Pareto dominance regardless of `--epsilon`, so step 09 builds its reference set with the Python ε-box filter under the campaign vector (plain union kept as `*_raw.set`) before computing metrics, and `scripts/main/extract_runtime_archive.py --merge --install` writes the step-10 reference set with the same filter. The merge, ε-filter, re-evaluate ordering therefore holds for every configuration by construction.

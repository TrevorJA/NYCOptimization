Primary research questions for this study. Numbering follows the manuscript (Section 1, P7).

1. Do policies within the FFMP rule structure exist that, relative to current operations evaluated under the same deeply uncertain conditions, simultaneously improve outcomes for multiple stakeholders (NYC water supply reliability, Montague and Trenton flow-target compliance, downstream flood exposure, and minimum reservoir storage) without degrading any other outcome beyond stated tolerances?

2. (Core) Does constructing the search ensemble by space-filling coverage of the hydrologic hazard space, rather than by independent and identically distributed sampling from the same stationary generator, change the robustness of the resulting Pareto-approximate policies under held-out, deeply uncertain re-evaluation?

The variable-resolution FFMP sweep (`ffmp_N` for N in {8, 10, 12}) is not a research question. It is a conditional SI extension run only on allocation left over after the campaign.
